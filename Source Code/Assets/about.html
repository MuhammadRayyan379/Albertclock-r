About Us

Crafting timeless luxury and horological excellence since 1990

CRAFTSMANSHIP & LEGACY

Our Heritage Since 1990

Founded over three decades ago, Alberto Watch Company has grown from a passionate horological workshop into a premier luxury watch retail, repair, and appraisal destination. We take pride in preserving classic watchmaking traditions while embracing modern innovation.

Every timepiece in our curated collection is rigorously verified by certified master watchmakers. Whether you are looking for an iconic vintage piece or a state-of-the-art smartwatch, our commitment to excellence ensures complete peace of mind.
