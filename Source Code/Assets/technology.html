Watch Technology

Engineering and innovation behind luxury horology

Watch Movements

The mechanisms powering the world's most prestigious timepieces

Mechanical Hand-Wind

Driven by a manual mainspring and escapement gear train. Pure traditional horology with zero batteries required.

Automatic Self-Winding

Features an oscillating weighted rotor that winds the mainspring naturally with everyday wrist movements.

Quartz Precision

Powered by a miniature battery sending current through a synthetic quartz crystal oscillating at 32,768 Hz.

Solar & Eco-Drive

Converts natural sunlight and indoor artificial light into clean electrical energy, stored in a rechargeable cell.

Smart Watch OS

Integrated multi-core digital processors, advanced health sensors, high-res OLED touchscreens, and wireless sync.

Chronograph Precision

Integrated stopwatch complication measuring split-second elapsed time with dedicated pushers and sub-dials.

Water Resistance Guide

Understanding atmospheric depth ratings for your timepieces

30M / 3 ATM

Splash resistant. Protected against everyday hand washing and light rain splashes.

50M / 5 ATM

Shallow water safe. Suitable for everyday showering and recreational swimming.

100M / 10 ATM

Watersports ready. Perfect for poolside swimming, snorkeling, and water sports.

300M+ / 30 ATM

Professional deep diver. Certified for high-pressure scuba diving and oceanic depths.
